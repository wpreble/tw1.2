# Privacy Policy

**The Way**
Last updated: March 11, 2026

---

## Overview

The Way ("we," "our," or "the app") is a spiritual formation tool that uses AI to help you engage with Scripture, prayer, and personal vision. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is handled.

---

## What We Collect

### Account Information
When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive:
- Your name (if provided by the sign-in provider)
- Your email address

This is used solely to create and manage your account.

### Conversation Data
Messages you send within the app are transmitted to our server and forwarded to OpenAI's API to generate responses. Conversations are **not stored on our servers** — they exist only on your device via local storage and are cleared when you clear your chat history or uninstall the app.

### Usage Data
We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or behavioral tracking data.

## Data Storage

- **On your device:** Chat history is stored locally using AsyncStorage and remains private to your device.
- **Authentication tokens:** Stored securely using the device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android).
- **Our servers:** We do not persistently store your messages or conversation history.

---

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except as required to operate the service (the third-party services listed above) or as required by law.
